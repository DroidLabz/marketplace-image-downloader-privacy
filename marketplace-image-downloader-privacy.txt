Marketplace Image Downloader – Privacy Policy

This browser extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share ANY personal data.

All processing happens entirely on the user’s device:
- Images are accessed only when the user explicitly clicks the download button.
- No data is ever sent to external servers.
- No analytics, no tracking, no logging.
- No cookies or storage are used.
- No third parties receive any information.

The extension performs a single local function: downloading full-resolution gallery images from Marketplace listings when the user requests it.

If you have questions or concerns, contact support through the Chrome Web Store listing.
